# 🎂 Celebration Zone - Cake Shop Website

Official website for Celebration Zone Bakery, Shillong.
Where every celebration starts with a cake!

## 🌟 About
Celebration Zone is a local bakery in Shillong that makes fresh custom cakes for birthdays, weddings, and all celebrations.
This website lets customers see our cakes and order instantly.

## 🚀 Features
- **Beautiful Cake Gallery** - Photos of our best cakes
- **Order on Zomato** - Direct link to order in 1 click
- **Our Location** - Find us on Google Maps
- **Contact & Timings** - Call us directly
- **Made for Mobile** - Works perfect on phone
